# T14: Constants and type aliases

Write a script for a small shop that works with prices in cents.

Declare a type alias `Cents` for integers, and constants for the tax rate in percent (10) and a free-shipping threshold (5000 cents), and a constant list of three product names.

Write:

- `formatCents(amount)`: formats an amount as dollars and cents, for example `1234` as `12.34` and `5` as `0.05`, using integer division and remainder
- `withTax(amount)`: the amount plus tax, rounded down to whole cents with integer division
- `shipping(amount)`: 0 when the amount is at least the free-shipping threshold, otherwise 500

In `main`, compute the total for a few items, and print the subtotal, the tax, the shipping cost and the grand total with `formatCents`.
